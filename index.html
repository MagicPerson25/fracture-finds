    Echoed Pairs

    Same character, two realities

    Reality Fracture prints familiar faces twice, as two separate cards showing who they became in different worlds. They aren't double-faced and don't need each other.

    Mechanics

    What's new in the rules

    The set's mechanics in plain words, with live examples from the set.

    New keyword action

    Empower Jace

    Put that many loyalty counters on a Jace token you control. If you don't control one, first create a blue Jace planeswalker token with "−1: Surveil 1" and "−3: Draw a card."

    The token starts at 0 loyalty, so the counters you put on it are all it has. Your real, nontoken Jace cards can't be empowered. Many cards then hand extra loyalty abilities to every planeswalker you control, so the little token can pick up strong options.

    New token

    Heartwood

    A red and green artifact token with "{T}: Add {R} or {G}." Unlike a Treasure, you keep it after using it, so it works as a permanent mana rock that also counts for artifact payoffs.

    Returning from Secrets of Strixhaven

    Prepare

    A Prepare card is a creature with a spell attached. While the creature is prepared, you may cast a copy of that spell, which unprepares it. If the creature leaves the battlefield first, the stored spell is gone.

    Returning keyword

    Flashback

    Cast the card from your graveyard for its flashback cost, then exile it. Timing rules stay the same.

    About this site

    Sources and notes

    Fracture Finds is an unofficial fan showcase of Reality Fracture. Card names, text, images and prices load live from the Scryfall API each time you open the page, so they always match Scryfall.

    How novelty is scored

    Tags come from Scryfall Tagger, the community project that labels what each card does. The tags were captured on , together with how many cards in all of Magic carry each tag. Each tag adds points based on how rare it is: a tag no other card has adds about 18, one shared by 5 cards adds 11, one shared by 20 adds 5, and tags on 60 or more cards add nothing. Tags about names, art or cycles are left out. On the cards, the score is shown as "Slightly unusual", "Unusual" or "Very unusual", and Tagger's shorthand tag names are translated into plain English. New tags added on Tagger after that date won't show here until the snapshot is refreshed.
